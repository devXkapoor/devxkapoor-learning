PART IV — DOING MORE THAN ONE THING AT ONCE

Six nodes, and the destination is the event loop — explained not as a diagram but as the specific thing it is: a userspace scheduler built on one system call, arrived at because two earlier designs failed for reasons you will be able to state numerically. Along the way, why a race condition is a hardware fact rather than a coding mistake, why SharedArrayBuffer needs Atomics, and what await actually costs.
