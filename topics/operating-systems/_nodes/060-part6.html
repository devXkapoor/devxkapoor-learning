PART VI — ISOLATION: WHO YOU ARE, WHAT YOU CAN SEE, WHAT YOU MAY USE

Five nodes. Identity and permission, then the three mechanisms — namespaces, cgroups, and layered filesystems — that together are a container. Node 28 assembles one from parts entirely already built, with no new concepts, which is the point: a container is not a technology, it is a configuration of the kernel you have been reading about for twenty-seven nodes.
